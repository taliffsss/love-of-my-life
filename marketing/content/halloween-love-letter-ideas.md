### Halloween love letter ideas: 12 spooky-sweet ways to say it

Halloween is a funny holiday for couples. It is all costumes and parties, and then somewhere in the middle you want to say something real.
A "happy Halloween" in the chat is gone in a minute. A letter is not.

This is a short guide to writing one that is playful, a little spooky and sincere, with examples you can copy, and one idea for how to
send it so they remember opening it.

### First, what makes a Halloween letter different?

Three things: **a little mischief** (a pun, a "boo"), **one real sentence** (the thing you mean), and **a small surprise** in how it
arrives. Keep it short. Four honest lines beat a page of jokes.

### 12 ideas to start from

1. **The "you haunt me" letter.** "The only thing haunting me this Halloween is how much I think about you."
2. **The costume memory.** Write about the best costume you saw them wear, and what you liked about them in it.
3. **The trick-or-treat list.** "Tricks: I stole your hoodie. Treats: everything else I owe you."
4. **A ghost story about us.** The tale of how you met, told like a spooky story with a sweet ending.
5. **The candy ranking.** Rank your favourite sweets, and put them at number two behind them.
6. **The spell.** "Ingredients: one late-night call, two bad jokes, one you. Result: I'm yours."
7. **The haunted house date.** Plan the night, and write the invitation as a letter.
8. **For the one far away.** If you are miles apart: "I can't knock on your door tonight, so I made one you can knock on."
9. **The first Halloween together.** What you remember, what you hope for the next.
10. **The mini love story.** Three short chapters: how we started, what I know now, what I want.
11. **The question letter.** Hide your message behind a question only you two know.
12. **The one-line card.** Seven words and a photo. Sometimes that is the whole letter.

### Three examples you can copy

> **Short:** Boo. Did I scare you? Good. The only thing haunting me is how much I like you. Happy Halloween, my favourite treat.

> **Sweet:** I don't need a costume to be myself around you. That's the best treat I got this year. Thank you for it.

> **Long distance:** I can't be at your door tonight, so here is one you can knock on. Behind it are my photos, our song and everything I'd say if I were there.

### How to write yours in 10 minutes

1. **Pick one idea** from the list.
2. **Write the sincere line first.** Then add the joke around it.
3. **Add one detail only you two know** (a place, a nickname, a date).
4. **Read it aloud.** If you would not say it, cut it.
5. **Decide how it arrives.** A card, a note in a bag of candy, a message, or something they open.

### Quick answers

**Is it okay to be cheesy?** Yes, if one line is real.
**How long should it be?** Four to eight lines is plenty.
**What if we are apart?** Write it as if you are at the door; the template idea above was made for that.
