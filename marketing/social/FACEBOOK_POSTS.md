# Facebook: 5 photo posts and 5 video posts

Domain on everything: **loveofmylife.love**. Images are in `marketing/out/social/`, videos in
`marketing/out/social/reels/` (both git-ignored; open `marketing/out/social/index.html` to browse the images).
Before the first post read "Before you post" in `PAGE_SETUP.md` (domain live, how-to-pay wording, own-address DNS,
under-18 rule). Every phone screen shown is the app's sample demo, not a real couple.

**Rules used here:** English first, then Taglish; one call to action; no invented numbers or testimonials; prices only as in
`config/saas.php` (Sweet ₱200/mo, Sweetheart ₱350/mo, Forever ₱4,999 once, photo pack ₱100); "free" always means "free
to build and preview". Post times are PHT guesses to test, not facts about your audience.
