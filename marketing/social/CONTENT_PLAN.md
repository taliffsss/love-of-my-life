# Content plan: launch and grow the Facebook Page and TikTok

Everything here is a **starting hypothesis**. Love of My Life has no customers or analytics yet, so there are no real
numbers about the audience in this kit and none are claimed. Run the plan for the first 30 days, then keep what the
Page Insights and TikTok Analytics prove.

## What we sell (all true to the app today)

One idea: **a private love-letter page: your photos, your song, your words, locked with a question only they can answer.**

## Audiences and what moves them

| Audience | What they're feeling | Angle | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OFWs** and their partners | Missing someone; time zones; wanting to show up on special days | "Miles apart, one link away." A letter to open any time. | Tender, never guilt-tripping |
| **Couples / lovers** | Monthsary pressure; wanting a gift that isn't generic | "Sweeter than a happy-monthsary chat." Add a letter every month | Playful, kilig |
| **Ages 16+** | First relationships, promposals, wanting to look sweet | "Your first monthsary." Free to build; scratch card to ask someone out | Light, wholesome, no pressure |
| **Event hosts** (weddings, debuts, christenings, showers) | Wanting a beautiful invitation that isn't a PDF | "Invite them in style." | Warm, a little formal |

**Messages, in order:** personal (your photos, song, words) → private (the question) → playful (10 ways to open it) →
easy (link or QR; free to build). Lead with feeling, not features; mention the price after the feeling.

## Voice: Lettie

Warm, gentle, a little shy, never pushy ("Buy now!!"), never mocking people who are single. EN by default; Taglish when
the post is about home, family or monthsaries. Use Lettie's stickers in comments and replies. See `../brand/LETTIE.md`.

## Honesty rules (so the Page earns trust)

- **No fake testimonials, reviews, "X couples already…" or screenshots of real people's pages.** There are none yet.
  Once there are, ask for permission and show only what the owner agrees to.
- The phone screens in the kit show **sample content** from the app's demo.
- Prices: only the ones in the plan cards. "From ₱200 a month". A year costs twelve months: never advertise a yearly discount or free months.
- Don't mention "own address" (`name.loveofmylife.love`): it is switched off in the app (feature flag `SUBDOMAINS_ENABLED`) until the flag is on.
- Don't say how to pay until checkout is live (`PAGE_SETUP.md`, "Before you post").
- No giveaways or contests until you've decided the prize and the rules (Meta and TikTok both require specific wording).

## Cadence

| | Facebook Page | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Per week | 5 posts (1 service, 1 audience, 1 engagement, 1 plan or tip, 1 story/reel) + daily Stories if you can | 4–5 posts (2 videos, 1 carousel, 1 reply-to-comment video) |
| Times to test (PHT) | 12:00–13:00 and 19:00–22:00; OFW posts at 20:00–22:00 (afternoon in the Gulf) | 18:00–22:00 weekdays; weekend mornings |
| Reply to comments | within a few hours; use the reply bank | reply to comments with a video when a good question comes in |

These are guesses to test, not facts about your audience. Compare two weeks of each slot, keep the winner.

## Occasion calendar (Philippines)

Valentine's Day (14 Feb) is the biggest gifting date: start the teaser the first week of February. Monthsaries land around the 14th and
the end of each month (the two most common dates, per `../copy/SOCIAL.md`): post a monthsary piece on the 12th and 27th.
Christmas and the "-ber" months bring OFWs home and make gifts and invitations timely. Weddings, debuts and christenings
are year-round, so keep one invitation post a month. Add anniversaries and birthdays as they appear in comments.

## What to measure (and what a good week means)

| Metric | Why | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Link clicks to the landing page | The only action that matters | Compare per post type |
| Sign-ups and first pages built | Do clicks become users | The app does not record where a visitor came from (no UTM or analytics code found), so compare daily sign-ups in the admin dashboard with the day's posts, and add `?utm_source=facebook` / `tiktok` links so a web-analytics tool can be added later |
| Saves and shares | A love-letter post is shared to someone | Higher than likes for this niche |
| Comment count on polls | Engagement | Poll cards should beat plain posts |
| Video: 3-second hold and completion | Hook strength (TikTok) | Rewrite the first line of videos that lose people early |

Decide at day 30: which of the four audiences clicks most, and put the next month's posts there. Paid boosts are
worth testing only after day 14, with the best organic post, to people 18+, and the honesty rules above.

## Content ideas bank (more than 30 days)

1. "Things to write in your letter" lists (first date, a promise, a memory, why now).
2. Template deep-dives: one post per template (Envelope, Scratch, Storybook, Memory, Timeline, Gift, Stars).
3. "Monthsary ideas" lists (by budget, by distance).
4. OFW homecoming countdown posts (December, "a letter for the airport").
5. "Reply with your love language" polls and "Which template are you?" quizzes.
6. Behind the scenes: Lettie, the colours, the making of the app.
7. Guides: "How to choose your secret question" (a question only they can answer, not one anyone can guess).
8. Seasonal: Valentine's, Christmas, New Year promises, Mother's/Father's Day letters.
9. Invitation inspiration: debut, christening, baby shower, wedding program.
10. Q&A posts answering the FAQ (privacy, edit after sending, photo limits).
