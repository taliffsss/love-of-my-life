# Love of My Life: social posts and ad copy

For Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. The audience is couples in the Philippines and Filipinos abroad, mostly 18–35,
who celebrate monthsaries, anniversaries, birthdays and Valentine's. Lettie, the brand's little love letter, is the
voice. Every line is in English and in Taglish; use whichever fits the page's audience.

**The idea in one line:** a love letter they unlock with a question only they can answer, with your photos, your song and
your words, opened in a way that feels like a gift.

**Messages, in order of importance:**
1. It's personal: your photos, your song, your letter.
2. It's private: locked with a question only they can answer.
3. It's playful: break a seal, scratch a card, turn the pages, light up the stars (10 templates).
4. It's easy to share: a link or a QR code on a card. Sweetheart and Forever pages get their own address.

## Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram)

Use the video or `feed-envelope.png`. Send the ad to the landing page.

**Primary text (pick one per ad set):**
1. "Got something sweet to say? Make them a page of their own: your photos, your song and a letter only they can open. 💌"
2. "Monthsary coming up? Give them a love letter they unlock with a question only the two of you know the answer to."
3. "Break a seal. Scratch a card. Turn the pages. 10 sweet ways for them to open your letter."
4. TL: "Sulat na may lock 🔒💌 Mabubuksan lang niya kapag nasagot niya ang tanong na kayo lang ang nakakaalam."

**Headlines (≤ 40 characters):**
- "A love letter only they can open"
- "Make them a page of their own"
- "10 ways to open a love letter"
- TL: "Sulat na para lang sa kanya"

**Description:** "Photos, your song and your letter. Free to try, from ₱200 a month." The preview is free, so this is
true.
**Button:** *Sign up* (cold audiences) or *Learn more* (retargeting).

## Keep it honest

- **Sample content.** The photos in the video and cards are illustrations and the letter is a sample; the video's end
  card says so. Don't present them as a real couple's page.
- **Prices.** The plans in the app: Sweet ₱200 a month (₱2,400 a year), Sweetheart ₱350 a month (₱4,200 a year),
  Forever ₱4,999 once; extra photos 12 for ₱100. A year costs twelve months: never advertise free months or a yearly
  discount. When you quote them, say "from ₱200 a month" and keep the free preview in front: "Try it free".
- **Own address.** "Own address" (`name.love.markanthonynaluz.com`) comes with Sweetheart and Forever; the video's end
  note says so.
- **Payments.** Don't claim online checkout until it is live; payments are confirmed by hand for now.
- **The domain must be live first.** Before posting, `love.markanthonynaluz.com` needs its DNS records and the 3.0 deploy.
